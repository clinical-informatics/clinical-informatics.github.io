## Go deeper

**If you want the canonical case study before moving on:**
- Obermeyer, Powers, Vogeli, Mullainathan, "Dissecting racial bias in an algorithm used to manage the health of populations," *Science*, October 2019. The paper this whole track keeps coming back to. Walks the analysis of a commercial population-health algorithm in production at scale, identifies the label as the source of bias, quantifies the effect (Black patients enrolled in care management would rise from 17.7% to 46.5% with a corrected label), and proposes the label fix. The publication is paywalled at *Science*; the accepted manuscript is broadly accessible via the authors' institutional pages, and the PubMed abstract carries the core finding. [pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31649194](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31649194/)

**If you want the clinical-algorithm review:**
- Vyas, Eisenstein, and Jones, "Hidden in Plain Sight: Reconsidering the Use of Race Correction in Clinical Algorithms," *NEJM*, August 2020. Free abstract at PubMed; the *NEJM* article itself is paywalled but a faculty-affiliated reader can usually access. The article catalyzed the post-2020 removal of race correction from major clinical algorithms (eGFR, VBAC, spirometry). The PSNet AHRQ commentary summarizes the cases for readers without *NEJM* access. [pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32853499](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32853499/) and [psnet.ahrq.gov/issue/hidden-plain-sight-reconsidering-use-race-correction-clinical-algorithms](https://psnet.ahrq.gov/issue/hidden-plain-sight-reconsidering-use-race-correction-clinical-algorithms)

**If you want to go significantly further into the formal theory:**
- Kleinberg, Mullainathan, and Raghavan, "Inherent Trade-Offs in the Fair Determination of Risk Scores," arXiv 2016. The formal impossibility result this track summarizes. Free on arXiv. Twenty pages, mostly accessible to a reader with introductory statistics. Establishes that calibration, equal false-positive rate, and equal false-negative rate cannot all hold simultaneously except when group base rates are identical. This is the result that means "fairness" must be a deliberate choice of criterion, not an aggregate property. Pair with Chouldechova's 2017 *Big Data* article on the COMPAS recidivism case for the criminal-justice instantiation of the same result. [arxiv.org/abs/1609.05807](https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.05807)
