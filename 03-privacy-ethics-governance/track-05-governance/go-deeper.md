## Go deeper

**If you want a real implementation study before moving on:**
- Sendak, Gao, Brajer, and Balu, "Real-World Integration of a Sepsis Deep Learning Technology Into Routine Clinical Care," JMIR Medical Informatics, 2020. Free on PMC. Describes Duke's Sepsis Watch deployment: the multidisciplinary team that built it, the integration with the existing rapid-response workflow, the monitoring plan, and the lessons that emerged. One of the few published examples of clinical-AI governance worked out in the open. [pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7391165](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7391165/)

**If you want the ethnographic view of the same case:**
- Elish and Watkins, "Repairing Innovation: A Study of Integrating AI in Clinical Care," Data & Society Research Institute, 2020. Free, Creative Commons license. An ethnographic study of the same Sepsis Watch deployment, written from the perspective of how it actually fit (and didn't fit) into the day-to-day work of the clinicians and nurses. The companion piece to the technical implementation paper; the technical paper says "this is what we built," the ethnographic study says "this is what it took to make it work in practice." [datasociety.net/library/repairing-innovation](https://datasociety.net/library/repairing-innovation/)

**If you want to go significantly further on the policy and ethics infrastructure:**
- The Office of the National Coordinator's HTI-1 Final Rule (2024) and its successor rules establishing transparency and governance requirements for "predictive decision support intervention" (any clinical AI in certified EHR technology). The rule introduces explicit governance expectations: documentation of inputs and outputs, performance metrics, intended use, and monitoring plans. Reading the rule is reading the federal government's emerging answer to "what should institutional governance of clinical AI look like?" Free on healthit.gov. Pair with the Coalition for Health AI (CHAI) Assurance Standards Guide for the institutional operationalization of these requirements. [healthit.gov/topic/laws-regulation-and-policy/health-data-technology-and-interoperability-certification-program](https://www.healthit.gov/topic/laws-regulation-and-policy/health-data-technology-and-interoperability-certification-program)
