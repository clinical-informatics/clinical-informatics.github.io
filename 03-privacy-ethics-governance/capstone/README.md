# Capstone: Course 03 Privacy, Ethics, and Governance

A research proposal walked through every framework in this course. You commit a written analysis at each step before the worked review is shown.

A regional health system has drafted a proposal: develop an AI tool to predict early sepsis on the inpatient wards, using five years of EHR data across the academic medical center and three community hospital partners. The system plans to partner with a national AI vendor, then eventually license the model out to other health systems. The proposal is sitting on the governance committee's docket for next week's meeting.

You are the clinical informatics representative on that committee. Your task is to produce a written review across five dimensions: privacy threat model, legal pathway, ethical analysis, equity review, and governance routing. Each dimension is a Socratic step. You commit to a written answer first, then the reveal opens with the worked analysis. At the end, you assemble the committee memo.

The format is the same as the 01-computational-thinking and 06-learn-fhir capstones: commit-then-reveal, with the work being the commitment, not the reveal. The reveal exists so you can compare your reasoning against a defensible version. The writing is what the course is checking.

**Prerequisite:** all five tracks in course 03.


**How to start:** `marimo run capstone/notebook.py`, or click the run button from the course home page.

## What the capstone is checking

- Can you name a clinical-data project's privacy threat model in concrete terms?
- Can you pick the right HIPAA pathway and identify the relevant other layers (state law, GDPR, 42 CFR Part 2)?
- Can you locate the project on the five-dimensional ethical-risk framework?
- Can you identify where bias might enter and what the institution should measure?
- Can you list the governance bodies that must approve, name the likely gaps, and propose what to do about them?
- Can you assemble that into a written committee memo your reviewers can act on?

If yes to all six, this course is done. The next stops are course 04 (clinical epidemiology), course 06 (FHIR), and course 12 (clinical decision support), where most of the equity questions in this course surface again in deployment-specific form.
