# Track 05: Population genomics, equity, and the future

The largest population-genomics resources of the past decade (All of Us, UK Biobank, MyCode, eMERGE) are reshaping what clinical genomics can do and what it cannot. The track presents each consortium, introduces polygenic risk scores at the concept level (and the open clinical-utility question for them), addresses the ancestry-vs-race distinction in genomics (cross-reference to Course 03 fairness and Course 09 bias), and addresses direct-to-consumer testing (23andMe, AncestryDNA) including the specific case of Ms. Reyes's pandemic-era 23andMe result and what it actually tells her about her RA risk (very little). The closing equity discussion documents the historical European-ancestry over-representation in genomic databases and what is being done about it.

**Prerequisites:** Tracks 01 through 04 of this course. Course 03 Track 4 (algorithmic fairness) and Course 09 Track 5 (bias entry points) are the cross-curriculum anchors for the equity discussion.

**How to start:** open `notebook.py`.

**Companion reading:** `go-deeper.md` in this folder.
