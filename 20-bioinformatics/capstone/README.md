# Capstone: Design a precision medicine program

A health system is launching a precision-medicine program: pharmacogenomic testing at the point of prescribing for a defined high-risk-medication list (azathioprine and TPMT, clopidogrel and CYP2C19, abacavir and HLA-B*5701, others). The reader is the clinical informaticist on the design team. The Socratic capstone walks six dimensions: the infrastructure required, the genomic-data standards that apply, how results are returned to patients, how the research and clinical streams coexist, what governance is required, and how equity considerations apply.

The capstone closes the curriculum. Each step is a commit-then-reveal pattern; the assembled reflection at the end is the artifact that says the reader has internalized the full vocabulary of every prior course.

**Prerequisites:** all five tracks in this course, plus the cross-references named in each step (Courses 03, 05, 06, 09 in particular).

**How to start:** open `notebook.py`. Marimo loads it in app mode.
