## Go deeper: Project management for informatics

**If you want the source document behind the iterative methodologies:**

- [Manifesto for Agile Software Development](https://agilemanifesto.org/) is the 2001 statement, by seventeen signatories including Kent Beck and Martin Fowler, of the four values that define Agile: individuals and interactions over processes and tools, working software over comprehensive documentation, customer collaboration over contract negotiation, responding to change over following a plan. One page, free, and the linked principles page adds the twelve principles behind the values. Reading it clarifies what the track asserts: Agile is a value system about discovering requirements by delivering, not a ceremony catalog. The Scrum Guide (scrumguides.org, also free) is the formalization of those values into the sprint structure the track describes.

**If you want a published worked example of health IT project management:**

- [Introduction of a Single Electronic Health Record for Maternity Units in Ireland: Outline of the Experiences of the Project Management Team](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10221488/) (JMIR Formative Research, 2023) is a project management team's first-person account of a national maternity EHR rollout: the governance board, train-the-trainer programs for roughly 2,500 staff, and big-bang go-live weekends supported by a 24/7 command center. The paper shows the track's concepts (governance, milestones, training calendars, go-live support) operating at national scale, including what the team would do differently. Free full text on PubMed Central.

**If you want a full implementation-planning resource to keep at hand:**

- [ONC Health IT Playbook, Section 1: Electronic Health Records](https://playbook.healthit.gov/playbook/electronic-health-records/) is the federal Office of the National Coordinator's free playbook section on the EHR lifecycle: contracts, selection, adoption and implementation, optimization, and data migration, with downloadable checklists and guides. The adoption-and-implementation material maps directly onto this track's planning concepts, and the optimization material previews the post-launch queue Track 8 covers. Written for provider organizations, so the advice assumes the committee and budget structures this course teaches.
