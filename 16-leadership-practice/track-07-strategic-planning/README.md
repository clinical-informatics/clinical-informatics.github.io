# Track 07: Strategic planning and IT portfolio management

The IT Steering Committee at Helios meets quarterly, and at the winter meeting, four months before the CMIO's congratulations email, the RA flare-risk CDS was one of 14 funding requests on the agenda. Requests at that table are judged on strategic alignment, not technical merit; the AI Governance Committee had already done the technical review. Strategic alignment asks whether the request traces to the institution's stated mission, where it sits in the IT portfolio, and whether its money arrives through the right cycle at the right time. Track 07 reconstructs how the RA-CDS won its funding quarter, and what the same machinery does to the requests that lose.

Five structures carry the track. Mission and vision sit at the top, and a request that can state its mission link in one sentence gives the committee a reason to rank it. SWOT analysis locates the institution before anyone plans for it; the worked Helios SWOT positions the RA-CDS against the system's real strengths (mature EHR, working governance) and real threats (alert-load burnout, ransomware). The 3-year roadmap with annual refresh separates sequenced commitments from a wish list. The capital-cycle calendar decides timing: requests submitted in fall fund the next fiscal year, and a request that misses the window waits a year. And the run/grow/transform portfolio view treats IT investments as a balanced, managed set rather than a first-come backlog, with kill and defer decisions as legitimate recurring outputs of governance.

The interactive is a portfolio-prioritization matrix over the eight scored requests on the Helios agenda. Setting the weights on strategic value, effort, and risk reduction, and setting the capital ceiling, re-ranks the portfolio reactively; the security hardening program, which treats no patient, tops the ranking the moment risk reduction is weighted seriously, and the portfolio view is what makes that argument visible. The track's artifact is the strategic-alignment statement for the RA-CDS (mission link, SWOT position, portfolio category, capital-cycle timing), which the capstone collects as section 8 of the implementation plan.
