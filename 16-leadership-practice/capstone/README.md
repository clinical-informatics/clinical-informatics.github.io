# Capstone: Assemble the RA-CDS implementation plan

A building capstone. The CMIO's email at the start of this course said "Congratulations. You're leading the implementation. Go-live is six months out." This capstone produces the document that email asks for: the implementation plan for the RA flare-risk alert (patient-view hook, 0.30 firing threshold, 90-day flare window, the 1,247-patient rheumatology RA panel at Helios Academic Medical Center). Ten sections of compact form fields assemble reactively into a single downloadable Markdown plan, and a one-page BLUF-structured executive summary derives automatically from the charter, budget, KPI, and pitch inputs, with its own download.

Each of the eight tracks contributed one section. Track 1 the project charter (section 1). Track 2 the RACI matrix and the canonical six-month timeline (sections 2 and 3). Track 3 the change-management plan, Kotter's eight steps applied to the rollout (section 4). Track 4 the KPI dashboard with target thresholds, selected from the 20-item catalog (section 5). Track 5 the budget and 5-year ROI, computed live from the canonical money facts ($240K one-time, $60K per year, $5K per flare averted, 3% discount rate) (section 6). Track 6 the executive-pitch summary in three slides (section 7). Track 7 the strategic-alignment statement and portfolio category (section 8). Track 8 the post-go-live operations plan (section 9). Section 10 is new here: a risk register with six predefined RA-CDS risks, each scored by likelihood and impact into a reactive ranked table, plus a seventh risk the reader adds.

Every field arrives carrying the worked entry its track produced, so the assembled plan is complete from the first render; editing any field updates the plan and the executive summary immediately. The capstone closes with an unrevealed reflection on which section the reader trusts least before handing the plan to a real CMIO.

**Prerequisites:** all eight tracks in this course. Course 12's capstone is the source of the CDS design brief this plan implements.

**How to start:** open `notebook.py`. Marimo loads it in app mode.
