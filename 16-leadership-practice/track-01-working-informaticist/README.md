# Track 01: The working clinical informaticist: roles, scope, day-to-day

The CMIO's email said three things: you are leading the implementation of the RA flare-risk alert from the Course 12 capstone, go-live is six months out, and you should ask for what you need. Replying well requires answers to three structural questions. Who is on the team, and what does each role do in a working week? Who reports to whom, and through which kind of line? And which committees hold authority over which decisions in the deployment? This track answers all three for Helios Academic Medical Center, the 720-bed Boston academic medical center where the deployment runs.

The track covers the seven informatics roles operationally (with a working-week sketch for the CMIO, the informatics director, and the EHR analyst), the reporting lines and the dual-reporting design behind the classic CMIO-vs-CIO tension, the six interfaces informatics serves (clinical operations, IT, research, finance, the C-suite, the board), the six Helios committees and the point at which each weighs in on the RA-CDS, and the who-decides-what matrix for clinical AI and CDS work. The interactive is a decision-routing exercise: six deployment decisions, each routed to its owner against a compact org chart, with feedback explaining the escalation paths.

The track's artifact is the **project charter**: purpose, scope, sponsor, stakeholders, governance path, and success criteria, worked for the RA-CDS. The capstone collects it as section 1 of the implementation plan.

**Prerequisites:** None within this course. Course 12 (the CDS design brief the running scenario continues); Course 0 Track 6 introduced the role titles this track makes operational.
