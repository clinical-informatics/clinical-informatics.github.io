# Go deeper: Decision analysis

**If you want to understand decision trees better before moving on:**

- [The Society for Medical Decision Making (SMDM)](https://smdm.org/) is the academic society for clinical decision analysis. The SMDM publishes tutorials and recorded short courses on decision-tree construction, Markov modeling, and probabilistic sensitivity analysis; the SMDM short-course pages are the standard introductory path.
- [The OpenMDAO and TreeAge Pro documentation](https://www.treeage.com/) covers the production tools used for clinical decision analysis at scale. TreeAge Pro is the dominant commercial tool; the academic-use literature uses R packages including `heemod` and `dampack` (both on CRAN, with vignettes).

**If you want to see this applied clinically:**

- [Trikalinos et al. "Decision-analytic modeling to evaluate benefits and harms of medical tests" (AHRQ 2014)](https://effectivehealthcare.ahrq.gov/) is the AHRQ methods report on applying decision analysis to clinical-test evaluation. The report is the most-cited methodological reference for decision trees in the AHRQ effective-health-care series and is freely available.
- [Detsky et al. "Primer on Medical Decision Analysis" (Medical Decision Making 1997)](https://journals.sagepub.com/home/mdm) is a classic series of pedagogical papers that walk decision-tree construction, sensitivity analysis, and Markov modeling on clinical examples. The series remains the standard pedagogical reference.

**If you want to go significantly further:**

- [Hunink et al., "Decision Making in Health and Medicine: Integrating Evidence and Values" (Cambridge University Press, 2nd ed)](https://www.cambridge.org/) is the standard graduate textbook for clinical decision analysis. The chapters on decision trees, Markov models, value of information, and probabilistic sensitivity analysis cover the full toolset a clinical informaticist working in this space would use.
