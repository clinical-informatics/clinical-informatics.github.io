# Go deeper: Decision curve analysis

**If you want to understand DCA better before moving on:**

- [Vickers and Elkin, "Decision curve analysis: a novel method for evaluating prediction models" (Medical Decision Making 2006)](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/0272989X06295361) is the foundational DCA paper. The paper introduces the net-benefit framework, the treat-all vs treat-none vs use-the-model curve construction, and the clinical interpretation. The paper is the right starting point.
- [The Decision Curve Analysis website at Memorial Sloan Kettering](https://www.decisioncurveanalysis.org/) is maintained by the Vickers group. The site hosts R, Stata, SAS, and Python implementations of DCA, with worked examples and an FAQ.

**If you want to see this applied clinically:**

- [Vickers, van Calster, Steyerberg, "A simple, step-by-step guide to interpreting decision curve analysis" (Diagnostic and Prognostic Research 2019)](https://diagnprognres.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/s41512-019-0064-7) is the open-access clinical-interpretation guide. The paper walks the DCA chart construction and interpretation on three published clinical examples and is the standard reference for clinicians learning to read DCA plots.

**If you want to go significantly further:**

- [Vickers, van Calster, Steyerberg "Net benefit approaches to the evaluation of prediction models, molecular markers, and diagnostic tests" (BMJ 2016)](https://www.bmj.com/content/352/bmj.i6) is the comprehensive methods paper covering net benefit, DCA, and the alternatives (relative utility, net reclassification index). The paper is the right deep reference for someone evaluating a model or test against DCA as part of methodological review.
