# Go deeper: Cost and utilization measures

**If you want to understand the cost-and-utilization vocabulary better before moving on:**

- [The Agency for Healthcare Research and Quality (AHRQ) Healthcare Cost and Utilization Project (HCUP)](https://hcup-us.ahrq.gov/) is the canonical reference for inpatient, ambulatory-surgery, and emergency-department utilization measurement in the US. The HCUP user-support materials define each utilization measure operationally and link to the SAS / R / Stata code that computes it.
- [The Kaiser Family Foundation State Health Facts](https://www.kff.org/statedata/) is the most accessible source for national and state-level utilization-and-cost benchmarks. Comparing an institution's utilization rate against the KFF benchmark for the same population is the standard sanity check.

**If you want to see this applied clinically:**

- [The Dartmouth Atlas of Health Care](https://www.dartmouthatlas.org/) documents geographic variation in cost and utilization across the US. The Atlas's published maps are the most-cited illustration of the fact that utilization differences across regions cannot be explained by clinical need alone.

**If you want to go significantly further:**

- [Health Affairs](https://www.healthaffairs.org/) is the policy-and-economics journal of record for US health-system research. The journal's free archive of editorials and the GrantWatch / Blog material includes most of the conceptual papers an informaticist will be asked to read in this space. The journal is paywalled for full research articles; many universities and large institutions provide access.
