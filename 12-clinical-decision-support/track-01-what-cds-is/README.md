# Track 01: What CDS actually is

Clinical decision support is a spectrum, not a single thing. The track defines the spectrum (passive alerts at one end, active recommendations in the middle, autonomous actions at the other end), names the published five-rights framework (right information, right person, right format, right channel, right time) that distinguishes a designed alert from a noisy one, and addresses the alert-fatigue / 97%-override-rate problem that is the default outcome of poorly designed CDS. The closing reframe is the cross-reference to Course 04: a CDS alert is a diagnostic test, and the low-prevalence-low-PPV math the 2x2 table chapter introduced is the math the alert is governed by.

**Prerequisites:** None within this course. Course 01 (computational thinking) and Course 04 Track 3 (the 2x2 table and PPV) are useful anchors.
