# Go deeper: Governance and the human side

**If you want to understand the CDS governance framework better before moving on:**

- [The Office of the National Coordinator (ONC) Clinical Decision Support and Health IT Safety pages](https://www.healthit.gov/topic/safety/clinical-decision-support) document the federal expectations for CDS governance in certified health IT. The SAFER Guides (a sub-set of the broader SAFER Guides for EHR safety) are the operational checklist most institutions use.
- [Wright et al., "Governance for clinical decision support: case studies and recommended practices from leading institutions" (JAMIA 2011)](https://academic.oup.com/jamia/article/18/2/187/725754) documents the governance structures at Brigham, Vanderbilt, and Kaiser. Reading the three case studies in parallel makes the institutional-design trade-offs concrete.

**If you want to see the equity discussion applied clinically:**

- [Vyas et al., "Hidden in Plain Sight: Reconsidering the Use of Race Correction in Clinical Algorithms" (NEJM 2020)](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMms2004740) is the load-bearing reference for the race-correction reconsideration of the past decade. The paper enumerates the clinical algorithms that historically embedded race as a variable (eGFR, VBAC calculator, ASCVD, others) and the equity arguments for removing race.
- [The 2024 ONC HTI-1 rule and its predictive-decision-support intervention requirements](https://www.healthit.gov/topic/laws-regulation-and-policy/health-data-technology-and-interoperability-certification-program) are the current federal regulatory layer governing AI-based CDS in certified EHRs. The "intervention risk management" requirements operationalize the equity, transparency, and monitoring expectations the track presents.

**If you want to go significantly further:**

- [The IMDRF (International Medical Device Regulators Forum) Software as a Medical Device framework](https://www.imdrf.org/) is the international consensus document on the SaMD risk classification (state of healthcare situation, significance of information). The FDA's Pre-Cert and Software Pre-Specifications guidance documents apply this framework to AI-based clinical-decision software specifically.
