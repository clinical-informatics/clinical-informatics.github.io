## Go deeper

**If you want to understand IG anatomy more deeply before moving on:**
- MITRE's "Reading a FHIR IG" module is the cleanest free walkthrough of how to actually pick up a published IG and find what you need: the navigation conventions of an IG website, the differential vs snapshot distinction, the must-support narrative pattern. Pair it with the HL7 FHIR R4 "ImplementationGuide" resource page (the metadata wrapper every IG carries) and the HL7 "Profiling FHIR" tutorial (the mechanics of profile authoring). [mitre.github.io/fhir-for-research/modules/data-modeling-reading-igs](https://mitre.github.io/fhir-for-research/modules/data-modeling-reading-igs), [hl7.org/fhir/R4/implementationguide.html](https://www.hl7.org/fhir/R4/implementationguide.html), and [hl7.org/fhir/R4/profiling.html](https://www.hl7.org/fhir/R4/profiling.html).

**If you want to see real IGs read end to end as they would be used clinically:**
- MITRE's "Real-World FHIR IGs" module surveys the landscape: which IGs cover which use cases, how they layer on US Core, and what to expect when integrating with each. After the survey, the HL7 mCODE Implementation Guide site is the worked example: it's well-bounded (oncology data), the narrative is clinically literate, and the profile inheritance is concrete (mCODE -> US Core -> FHIR core). Start with mCODE's "Background and Approach" tab, then walk one profile in full (Primary Cancer Condition is the canonical first read). [mitre.github.io/fhir-for-research/modules/data-modeling-real-world-igs](https://mitre.github.io/fhir-for-research/modules/data-modeling-real-world-igs) and [hl7.org/fhir/us/mcode/](https://www.hl7.org/fhir/us/mcode/).

**If you want to go significantly further (i.e., author your own IG):**
- MITRE's "Writing a FHIR IG" module walks the IG authoring workflow at the right altitude for someone designing a small custom IG (which is exactly what Track 4's gap-analysis capstone implies). The actual open-source toolchain is FHIR Shorthand (FSH) plus the SUSHI compiler plus the HL7 IG Publisher: you write profiles in compact FSH syntax, SUSHI compiles them into FHIR JSON, and the IG Publisher generates the publishable IG website. You can author a small custom IG in an afternoon once you've internalized the mechanics. [mitre.github.io/fhir-for-research/modules/data-modeling-writing-igs](https://mitre.github.io/fhir-for-research/modules/data-modeling-writing-igs), [build.fhir.org/ig/HL7/fhir-shorthand/](https://build.fhir.org/ig/HL7/fhir-shorthand/), and [fshschool.org](https://fshschool.org).
