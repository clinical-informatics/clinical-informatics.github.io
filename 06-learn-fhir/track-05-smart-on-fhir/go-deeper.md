## Go deeper

**If you want to understand the SMART App Launch flow more deeply before moving on:**
- MITRE's two SMART on FHIR modules ("Introduction" and "Technical Details") are the gentlest free walkthrough of the launch flow, the scope vocabulary, and the launch context, written for a clinical-informatics audience. After those, the HL7 SMART App Launch implementation guide is the canonical specification: the launch sequence, the scope vocabulary, the token-response shape, the conformance requirements for SMART-enabled servers. Read the IG's "App Launch" page first for the conceptual flow, then the "Scopes and Launch Context" page, then the "Backend Services" page if you'll be writing services that authenticate without a user. [mitre.github.io/fhir-for-research/modules/smart-on-fhir-intro](https://mitre.github.io/fhir-for-research/modules/smart-on-fhir-intro), [mitre.github.io/fhir-for-research/modules/smart-on-fhir-tech](https://mitre.github.io/fhir-for-research/modules/smart-on-fhir-tech), and [hl7.org/fhir/smart-app-launch/](https://hl7.org/fhir/smart-app-launch/).

**If you want to see this applied to real apps in a real sandbox:**
- The SMART Health IT sandbox at `launch.smarthealthit.org`. Free, publicly accessible, runs every SMART launch variant. The "App Launcher" page lets you launch any registered SMART app against synthetic patient data, end to end (you click a button, the EHR-launch flow fires, your test app receives the launch context and a real token, and can make FHIR queries). Their sample-apps repo has working code for common patterns. [launch.smarthealthit.org](https://launch.smarthealthit.org/), [github.com/smart-on-fhir/sample-apps-stu3](https://github.com/smart-on-fhir/sample-apps-stu3), and the parent organization at [smarthealthit.org](https://smarthealthit.org).

**If you want to go significantly further on CDS Hooks and Bulk Data:**
- MITRE's CDS Hooks modules (Introduction, Types of CDS Hooks, Technical Example) and FHIR Bulk Data module pair clinical-research framing with concrete request/response walkthroughs. The standalone specs at `cds-hooks.hl7.org` and the HL7 Bulk Data IG are the canonical references; the MITRE pages are the gentler on-ramp. Both topics are deepened in Course 12 (Clinical decision support). For SMART app development end-to-end, the open-source `fhirclient` Python library is a worked example of the OAuth flow plus FHIR API patterns. [mitre.github.io/fhir-for-research/modules/new-cds-hooks-intro](https://mitre.github.io/fhir-for-research/modules/new-cds-hooks-intro), [mitre.github.io/fhir-for-research/modules/new-cds-hooks-types](https://mitre.github.io/fhir-for-research/modules/new-cds-hooks-types), [mitre.github.io/fhir-for-research/modules/new-cds-hooks-tech](https://mitre.github.io/fhir-for-research/modules/new-cds-hooks-tech), [mitre.github.io/fhir-for-research/modules/new-bulk-fhir](https://mitre.github.io/fhir-for-research/modules/new-bulk-fhir), [cds-hooks.hl7.org](https://cds-hooks.hl7.org/), [hl7.org/fhir/uv/bulkdata](https://hl7.org/fhir/uv/bulkdata/), and [github.com/smart-on-fhir/client-py](https://github.com/smart-on-fhir/client-py).
