# Capstone: Produce two communication artifacts from the Course 12 CDS design brief

A building capstone. The reader is handed the CDS design brief that Course 12's capstone produced (the Reyes-style RA-flare alert), and uses it to assemble two communication artifacts: a **2-minute verbal pitch for a CMO** (BLUF-structured: ask, finding, implication, action, risk and mitigation) and a **one-page visual summary for clinical staff** (situation, population, threshold and override, success criterion, illustrative chart). Both artifacts are assembled reactively from the reader's form inputs in the notebook; both are exported as downloadable Markdown files an actual clinical-informatics implementation team could carry into the meetings the artifacts are designed for.

The capstone is the synthesis of the five tracks. Audience analysis (Track 01) determines that the same brief produces two artifacts because the two audiences (executive, clinical) need differently-shaped versions of the same facts. Plain-English writing (Track 02) determines the sentence-level craft of the CMO pitch. Narrative structure (Track 03) determines the BLUF and FIR shape of the pitch and the situation-population-threshold-override-success shape of the clinical summary. Visual presentation (Track 04) determines the chart shown in the clinical summary, with its annotations matched to the clinical audience. Vendor and AI-team communication (Track 05) is not directly carried in these two artifacts but is the operational backdrop for the design-brief's underlying CDS work.

**Prerequisites:** all five tracks in this course. Course 12 is the source of the CDS design brief.

**How to start:** open `notebook.py`. Marimo loads it in app mode.
