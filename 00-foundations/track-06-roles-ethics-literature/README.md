# Track 06: Roles, ethics, and where the literature lives

Track 05 named the actor groups. This track zooms inside them. Almost every actor in the U.S. health system employs clinical informaticists; the title varies by setting; the day-to-day work varies more. The track is a working taxonomy of titles and two pieces of professional infrastructure: the **AMIA pathway** for getting into the field and the **publication venues** where the field's work lives.

The track moves through four pieces.

1. **The working titles.** CMIO (the senior physician executive responsible for clinical use of information systems), CNIO (the senior nursing informatics executive), CRIO (the senior research-information executive), clinical informaticist (the non-executive title), clinical data scientist (the increasingly common adjacent role), and health IT (the broader information technology staff). What each role actually does day to day, and where the boundaries between them sit.
2. **Training pathways.** AMIA 10x10 (the widely-taken introductory program), master's and PhD programs in clinical and biomedical informatics, ACGME-accredited Clinical Informatics fellowships, the ABPM Clinical Informatics subspecialty board exam, and the parallel nursing and other allied pathways.
3. **The ethical premise of the field.** A short, orienting paragraph. Course 03 (privacy, ethics, and governance) is where the field treats ethics as serious work; for Course 0 the goal is to name the substrate.
4. **Where the field publishes.** The five core venues: JAMIA, JAMIA Open, *Applied Clinical Informatics*, the AMIA Annual Symposium proceedings, and *BMC Medical Informatics and Decision Making*. The adjacent venues (JBI, *Health Affairs*, *JAMA Health Forum*, *NEJM Catalyst*, *MMWR*) named alongside. The conferences worth knowing about (AMIA Symposium, AMIA Informatics Summit, HL7 FHIR DevDays, OHDSI Symposium, HIMSS).

The track closes with a *where would this paper land* exercise across four hypothetical submissions, with the trade-offs that drive each choice named alongside the answer.
