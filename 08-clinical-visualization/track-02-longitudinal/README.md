# Track 02: Longitudinal and time-series data

Disease activity over time. Ms. Reyes's CRP and ESR are loaded from the actual lab series in `start-here/patients/elena-reyes/labs.csv` and rendered as a four-year trajectory. The track addresses three decisions that every longitudinal chart faces: whether to show a reference-range band, whether to annotate the medication changes that explain inflections in the curve, and whether to smooth the line. Each decision is shown with the chart it produces, and the reader can toggle the band, the annotations, and the smoothing in the notebook.

**Prerequisites:** Track 01 of this course.

**How to start:** open `notebook.py`.

**Companion reading:** `go-deeper.md` in this folder.

**What's next:** Track 03 (Visualizing uncertainty).
