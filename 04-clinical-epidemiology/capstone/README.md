# Capstone: course 04

A staff rheumatologist hands you a 4-page summary of a community-hospital RA registry and asks for a defensible analysis of whether TNFi increases serious infection risk, in time for the next quality committee. Your job: identify the three biggest threats to validity in a naive analysis, propose mitigations for each, and write the one-paragraph analysis plan you would actually run.

The capstone is Socratic. Six commit-and-reveal steps pull together the course: define the question (Track 1), name confounding (Track 2), name selection bias and loss to follow-up (Track 2 + Track 5), name non-proportional hazards (Tracks 4 + 5), propose mitigations, and assemble the plan. The final cell auto-assembles your six written answers into the memo you would send to the committee.

**Prerequisite:** all five tracks of this course. Track 1 (incidence rate, HR), Track 2 (bias, DAGs, the three covariate roles), Track 4 (CI vs p-value framing), and Track 5 (study designs, Kaplan-Meier, the PH assumption) are the vocabularies the capstone pulls from.
