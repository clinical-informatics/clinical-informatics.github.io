# Capstone: Apply the policy framework to three real-shaped scenarios

A Socratic policy-analysis exercise. The reader works through three scenarios that span the policy framework the course has built. **Scenario A**: a breast-cancer survivor relocating across the country cannot get her records released from her former oncology practice for transmission to her new oncologist; her requests are stalled in a paper-based workflow and the patient portal does not expose the labs or imaging she needs. **Scenario B**: a university faculty researcher with IRB approval to study RA outcomes at an affiliated health system is quoted $40,000 for the data extraction, with a $5,000 discount conditioned on a publication-rights surrender; internal researchers at the same system receive the same extractions at no charge. **Scenario C**: a health system trying to integrate a third-party care-management app with their certified EHR is faced with a $35,000 vendor "API integration certification fee," a 30% marketplace revenue share on app subscriptions, withheld sub-resource documentation, and a suggestion that the system buy the vendor's own care-management module instead.

For each scenario, the reader writes three substantive answers: which policy framework applies, what that framework requires, and what recourse exists. The reactive reveal compares the reader's analysis with how a policy-fluent reviewer would think through the same scenario. The final cell assembles the nine answers and a reflection into a downloadable Markdown policy brief.

Each question is gated by `mo.stop` at 30 characters so that the reader commits a substantive answer before the analytical reveal.

**Prerequisites:** all five tracks in this course.

**How to start:** open `notebook.py`. Marimo loads it in app mode.
