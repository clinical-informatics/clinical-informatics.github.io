## Go deeper: The international landscape

**If you want the GDPR text for the article that governs health data:**

- [Article 9 of the GDPR (Processing of special categories of personal data)](https://gdpr-info.eu/art-9-gdpr/) is the Article that singles out health data, genetic data, and biometric data as warranting additional protection beyond the general lawful-basis framework of Article 6. The gdpr-info.eu site, maintained by Intersoft Consulting, presents the official text article by article with recitals linked from each. It is the reference for any question about what GDPR actually says about health data, and is the EU comparator to the U.S. HIPAA Privacy Rule from Course 03.

**If you want a single-payer system with a national identifier in operation:**

- [NHS England's Digital transformation page](https://www.england.nhs.uk/digitaltechnology/) is the landing page for the NHS's current digital programme, including the Federated Data Platform, the Single Patient Record initiative, and the NHS App. NHS England (which absorbed NHS Digital in 2023) is the operational example of how a national health system built on a universal NHS Number can mandate interoperability through procurement rather than through a Cures-style information-blocking statute.

**If you want to go significantly further into the EU regulatory frontier:**

- [The European Commission's European Health Data Space Regulation page](https://health.ec.europa.eu/ehealth-digital-health-and-care/european-health-data-space-regulation-ehds_en) explains the 2025 EHDS regulation, which is the EU's analog to the federal-policy framework Tracks 02 and 03 cover, with the addition of a formal separation between primary and secondary use of health data. The page tracks the EHDS implementation timeline (entry into force in 2025, full operational phases at 2027, 2029, 2031, 2035) and links to the implementing acts that will specify the European Electronic Health Record Exchange Format and the Health Data Access Bodies in each member state.
