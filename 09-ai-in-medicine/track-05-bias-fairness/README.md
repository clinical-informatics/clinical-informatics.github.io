# Track 05: Bias, fairness, and clinical risk

Bias in a clinical AI model enters at one of four points: in the training data, in the labels, in the features, or in the deployment context. The track addresses each entry point with a concrete clinical example, then demonstrates subgroup performance on a synthetic model (the same model can have an overall AUC of 0.80 and a Black-patient AUC of 0.65). The fairness-metric trade-off is presented next: demographic parity, equalized odds, and calibration parity cannot all be satisfied simultaneously except in degenerate cases. The track closes with a vendor-evaluation checklist of the questions a clinical informaticist should ask before adoption.
