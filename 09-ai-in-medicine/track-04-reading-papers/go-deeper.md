# Go deeper: Reading an AI paper critically

**If you want the canonical appraisal framework:**

- [TRIPOD-AI](https://www.equator-network.org/reporting-guidelines/tripod-statement/) is the reporting guideline for clinical AI prediction models (Collins, Moons, and colleagues; updated 2024 to cover AI specifically). The checklist itself is the most concise version of the five-dimension framework the track presents and is the document a peer reviewer would have open while assessing a manuscript.
- [PROBAST](https://www.equator-network.org/reporting-guidelines/probast/) is the companion risk-of-bias tool. PROBAST covers participants, predictors, outcome, and analysis as the four risk-of-bias domains; reading TRIPOD-AI and PROBAST together is the standard appraisal workflow.

**If you want to see published examples of the framework being applied:**

- [Wynants et al. (BMJ 2020)](https://www.bmj.com/content/369/bmj.m1328) is the systematic review of COVID-19 prediction models that found nearly all were at high risk of bias. The paper is the published worked example of TRIPOD-AI + PROBAST applied at scale and is freely accessible at the BMJ.
- [Park and Han (Radiology 2018)](https://pubs.rsna.org/doi/10.1148/radiol.2017171920), "Methodologic Guide for Evaluating Clinical Performance and Effect of Artificial Intelligence Technology for Medical Diagnosis and Prediction," is a free open-access methods paper that walks through the appraisal framework specifically for clinical AI in radiology. The guidance generalizes to most clinical AI contexts.

**If you want to go significantly further:**

- [Sendak et al., "Real-World Integration of a Sepsis Deep Learning Technology Into Routine Clinical Care: Implementation Study" (JMIR Med Inform 2020)](https://medinform.jmir.org/2020/7/e15182/) is the published implementation account of the Duke Sepsis Watch model. The paper documents every appraisal dimension the track presents and adds the implementation considerations (workflow integration, alerting, governance) that the appraisal framework alone does not cover. It is the most cited single example of a clinical AI deployment that took the appraisal questions seriously.
