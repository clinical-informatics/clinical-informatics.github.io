# Track 01: Structured vs unstructured data

A clinical encounter produces both structured data (the coded fields in the EHR) and unstructured data (the narrative note). The two are not interchangeable. Most clinical reasoning, most context, most uncertainty, and most of the texture that gives a chart its clinical meaning lives in the note rather than in the fields. This track addresses the categorical reality of the distinction with a worked side-by-side of one of Ms. Reyes's encounters, presents the information-loss problem that follows when only structured fields are queried, and frames the rest of the course as the toolset that bridges the two representations.

**Prerequisites:** None within this course. Course 02 Track 1 (data types) is the formal vocabulary anchor.
