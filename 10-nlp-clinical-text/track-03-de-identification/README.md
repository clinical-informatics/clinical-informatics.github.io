# Track 03: De-identification

Clinical notes carry patient-identifying information embedded in narrative prose. Sharing the notes for research, quality improvement, or vendor evaluation requires de-identification: the removal or transformation of identifying spans so the residual text can be released without violating HIPAA. The track reviews the HIPAA Safe Harbor 18 identifiers in operational form, presents the three common approaches (rule-based, ML-based, hybrid), shows a before-and-after de-identification of a Reyes note, and addresses the residual risk that quasi-identifiers (ZIP code, date of birth, gender combinations) can re-identify a patient even after Safe Harbor compliance.

**Prerequisites:** Track 01 of this course; Course 03 Track 2 (HIPAA and the de-identification standards).

**How to start:** open `notebook.py`.
